# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 20, 2024**

PikCal ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- **Email address** - Collected through Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In for account identification
- **Authentication tokens** - Securely stored to maintain your session

### Health & Fitness Data
To provide personalized nutrition tracking, we collect:
- Gender, height, weight, and birth date
- Weight goals and target weight
- Workout frequency and diet preferences
- Daily calorie and macronutrient goals

### Food & Meal Data
- Photos of meals you scan (processed for nutritional analysis)
- Food items and their nutritional information
- Meal history and daily intake records

### Usage Data
- App preferences (language, units)
- Feature usage patterns (anonymized)

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- **Provide nutritional analysis** - Your meal photos are analyzed using AI to estimate calories and macronutrients
- **Calculate personalized goals** - Your health data helps us recommend appropriate calorie and macro targets
- **Sync across devices** - Your data is securely stored to enable access from any device
- **Improve our service** - Anonymized usage data helps us enhance the app

## Data Storage & Security

- Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase
- All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
- We do not sell your personal information to third parties
- Meal photos are processed for analysis and stored securely

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** your data through the app
- **Delete** your account and all associated data via Settings > Delete Account
- **Export** your data by contacting support

## Data Retention

- Your data is retained as long as your account is active
- Upon account deletion, all your data is permanently removed within 30 days
- We do not retain meal photos after nutritional analysis

## Children's Privacy

PikCal is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
