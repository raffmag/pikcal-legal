🚀 Getting Started
Simply take a photo of your meal and our AI instantly identifies the foods, estimates portions, and calculates calories and macros (protein, carbs, fat). Your meals are saved automatically so you can track your daily intake and progress toward your goals.
Our AI provides estimates that are typically within 10-20% of actual values for common foods with clear visibility. Accuracy depends on image quality, food visibility, and portion clarity. For the best results:
  • Take photos from above with good lighting
  • Make sure all foods are visible
  • Use the correction feature if something looks off
Remember, these are estimates to help you track trends, not exact measurements.
Your goals are personalized based on the information you provide during setup: age, gender, height, weight, activity level, and goal (lose, maintain, or gain weight). We use scientifically-backed formulas to calculate your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE), then adjust based on your target. You can always modify your goals in Settings.

⭐ Premium & Subscription
Premium unlocks:

Subscriptions are managed through Apple:
  • Open Settings on your iPhone
  • Tap your Apple ID at the top
  • Tap Subscriptions
  • Select PikCal and tap Cancel
You'll keep Premium access until the end of your billing period.
Since subscriptions are processed through Apple, refund requests must go through them. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund for any recent purchase.
Go to Settings → Restore Purchases in the app. This will sync your subscription status with Apple. If it still doesn't work, make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used for the purchase, then try again. Still having issues? Contact us.

🛠 Troubleshooting
Make sure PikCal has camera permission:
  • Go to iPhone Settings → PikCal
  • Enable Camera access
  • Restart the app
Try these steps:
  • Force close and reopen the app
  • Check your internet connection
  • Restart your iPhone
  • Update to the latest version from the App Store
If problems persist, contact support.
Scan failures are usually due to:
  • Poor connection — Try on a stable WiFi network
  • Image quality — Ensure good lighting and focus
  • Server issues — Wait a few minutes and try again
If you consistently see errors, please let us know.
