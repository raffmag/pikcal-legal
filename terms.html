1. The Service

PikCal is a nutrition tracker. You may photograph a plate, scan a barcode, photograph a nutrition table, or type a short description. The Service returns estimated calories and macronutrients (protein, carbohydrates, fat, and often fibre) against a plan calculated from information you provide.

Goals are calculated from the body figures you enter at set-up. Inaccurate figures produce an inaccurate plan.

3. Free tier and Pro

The free tier of the Service includes, on iOS, a daily allowance of AI scans (currently three per day) and, on Android, barcode lookup, typed meals, and fourteen days of history. Photo scan on Android is a Pro feature. Both platforms include goals, the Today view, and the ability to correct meals.

Pro includes unlimited photo scans, full history, saved foods, and the analysis features that ship with the current version. The in-application paywall is the live description of Pro.
