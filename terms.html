02What PikCal does

PikCal is a nutrition tracker for iOS and Android. You photograph a plate, scan a barcode, photograph a nutrition table, or type a sentence. The app returns estimated calories and macros — protein, carbs, fat, and often fiber — against a plan built from your body, goal and pace.

Snap

A plate, a pack, a label.

Track

Remaining calories and macros on Today. History with photos.

Correct

Edit foods, portions and values whenever the estimate is off.

05Not medical advice

PikCal is a tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. AI estimates can miss foods, misread portions, and sit 10–20% off even on a clear plate. Talk to a clinician before a significant diet change, and do not rely on the app alone to manage a health condition.

Optional Apple Health and Health Connect connections follow the same rule: convenience, not care.
