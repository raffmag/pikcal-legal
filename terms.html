📸 What PikCal Does

PikCal is a nutrition tracking app that uses artificial intelligence to analyze photos of your meals and estimate their nutritional content.

    Snap Your Meals

    Take a photo of any food and our AI identifies ingredients and portions

    Track Your Progress

    Monitor daily calories, protein, carbs, and fat against personalized goals

    Reach Your Goals

    Whether losing weight, building muscle, or maintaining - we calculate your targets
