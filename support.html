🛠 Quick Troubleshooting

Try these steps before contacting support:

  • 1

    Restart the app

    Force close PikCal and reopen it. This fixes most temporary issues.

  • 2

    Check your internet connection

    Scanning requires an internet connection for AI analysis.

  • 3

    Update to the latest version

    Open the App Store and check for updates to PikCal.

  • 4

    Check app permissions

    Go to Settings > PikCal and ensure Camera access is enabled.
