01In short

  • We collect what we need to track your nutrition and keep your account.
  • A meal photo or typed description is sent to OpenRouter (then Google and/or OpenAI) only after you tap Allow in the app.
  • Height, weight, age, calories and dates stay on the phone when we write your plan. The model is not shown a number.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can export or delete everything from Settings.
  • Usage analytics is optional. Crash reports help us fix the app.

02What we collect

Only what the product needs to work.

Account

Email from Sign in with Apple or Google, so we can identify your account and sync.

Body metrics

Height, weight, age and sex, used on-device to calculate calorie and macro targets. These numbers are stored on your account for the signed-in experience. They are not sent to the plan-writing models.

Fitness profile

Activity level, weight goal, pace, diet style, and the qualitative answers you give in onboarding.

Meals

Photos you scan, typed descriptions, barcode lookups, identified foods, portions, calories and macros, plus later edits.

Preferences

Language, units, appearance, and portion display.

Device

Model, OS version and app version, for crash reports and support. Not your meal content.

03How AI sees a meal

Scanning a plate, a nutrition label, or a typed description uses cloud models. Nothing is sent until you allow it in the app (Allow / Not now). You can change this later in Settings.

  1. Only the cropped photo, or the text you typed, is uploaded.
  2. OpenRouter routes the request to Google and/or OpenAI models.
  3. Estimates for foods, portions, calories and macros are saved to your account.
  4. The photo is stored so you can review and edit the meal later.

We do not sell this data. We share it with the processors listed below so the feature can run, and only after in-app Allow. Model providers process inputs under their own policies. Some providers may use inputs to improve their models. We do not train our own models on your meals. We do not send weight, height, age, calories or dates to those models.

Barcode lookups for packaged foods use Open Food Facts (and similar public catalogs). That path does not send a meal photo to the AI models.

04Your plan

Calorie and macro targets are calculated on your phone from the body figures you entered. If you allow plan copy, qualitative onboarding answers (diet style, obstacles, what already works) may be sent through OpenRouter to Google and OpenAI so the app can write the words of the plan. The model is never shown a number and is instructed never to write one. The phone substitutes the real figures locally.

Qualitative plan sentences may be stored in a shared, de-identified cache so the same wording can be reused. That cache is not keyed to your account. Deleting your account does not wipe every cached sentence.

05Health data

Optional, and off until you connect it.

Apple Health · iOS

If you connect it: we can read weight and active calories, and write logged nutrition back to HealthKit. You choose the types. You can revoke in Apple Health.

Health Connect · Android

The Android analogue of Apple Health. We do not use Google Fit. Reads (today’s steps, active calories, latest weight) stay on the device and are not uploaded to PikCal servers or sent to analytics. If “Sync meals” is on, we write a nutrition record for each saved meal. Disconnect and we stop writing.

Health numbers used to compute your plan stay on the phone. They are not sent to OpenRouter, Google or OpenAI.

06Analytics & crashes

PostHog

Product events (meal logged, edited, deleted, screens, app open). An anonymous id, then your PikCal user id after sign-in. No email, no meal photo, no ingredient list. Session replay is off. Opt out in Settings → usage statistics.

Firebase Crashlytics

When the app crashes: stack trace, app state, device model, OS, locale. We do not put meal photos or email in crash reports. On Android we never attach your user id to Crashlytics.

07Notifications

Meal reminders are scheduled on your device. They do not need our servers.

On iOS, optional remote notifications (tips, product updates) may use Firebase Cloud Messaging. We then store a device token — not your email — and delete it when you turn notifications off or delete the account. Android does not currently send remote push.

10For European users (GDPR)

Legal bases

Contract, for the core tracker. Consent, for AI analysis, plan copy, analytics and optional notifications. Legitimate interests, for security and fraud prevention.

Transfers

Some processors (OpenRouter, Google, OpenAI, PostHog, Firebase) may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where applicable, and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Retention

Account data while the account is open. Crash logs about 90 days. Analytics about 12 months. After deletion, personal data is removed from active systems promptly, and from backups within 30 days.

Requests

Access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, withdraw consent: support@pikcal.app. You may also lodge a complaint with your local authority.

11Account deletion

Settings → Delete account. We permanently remove:

  1. Profile and email
  2. Meal scans, food entries and stored photos
  3. Goals, preferences and onboarding answers tied to you
  4. Push tokens and analytics associations
  5. The authentication account. Apple Sign-In tokens are revoked.

Store purchases remain with Apple or Google — we cannot erase a store receipt. The de-identified plan-copy cache is not wiped per user. Deletion is irreversible.

12Children

PikCal is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think we have, write to support@pikcal.app and we will delete it.

13Updates

We may revise this policy as the product changes. Material changes will be flagged in the app. The date at the top is the latest revision.
